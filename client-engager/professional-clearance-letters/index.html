Professional clearance letters.

When you take on a client who has used another accountant, you send their previous accountant a professional clearance letter, with the client's consent. It sets out everything you need from them in order to take over and do the accounts.

Client Engager only includes each point in the letter when the client has one of that point's services ticked. These are the points in our letter, and the services each one applies to.
