Phase 1

Preparation

Assigned to: Bookkeeper/admin assistant

Checklist points

1

All subcontractor invoices received for the period.

2

New subcontractors verified with HMRC and their deduction rate recorded.

3

Deductions calculated correctly (labour only, excluding materials and VAT).

Phase 2

Review

Assigned to: Client manager

Checklist points

1

CIS return reviewed against the invoices and payments made.

Phase 3

Filing

Assigned to: Client manager

Checklist points

1

CIS300 monthly return filed by the 19th.

2

Payment and deduction statements sent to subcontractors.

3

Client told the amount due to HMRC and the payment reference.

4

CIS suffered recorded on the EPS where applicable (limited companies).
