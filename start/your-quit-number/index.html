Your quit number.

The monthly figure you can live off. Hit it, and you can hand in your notice.

What it is

01

The number that sets you free

  • What do you need each month to live, before you quit?
  • That figure is your quit number. For most people in Start it is around £5k a month.
  • It turns "one day I will go on my own" into a target with a finish line.

The exercise

02

List every cost, line by line

  • Mortgage or rent, bills, car, food, family, savings.
  • Look up the real cost of each one. No guesses.
  • Add it up. That is your quit number.

03

Then your dream-life number

  • Same exercise, but if money were no object.
  • The house, the car, the holidays, gifts for family.
  • You don't need it yet. It's what keeps you going on the hard weekends.

04

Use the milestone calculator

  • It turns your living costs into your quit number for you.
  • Add yours: link to the milestone calculator.

My quit number

June 2023

The day the practice overtook my job

  • 6 months after my first day on the high street.
  • About £5k a month, from weekends alone.
  • I'd just applied for a mortgage, so I stayed a bit longer. Plan for things like that.

Your task

Write your number down.

  • Work out your quit number and your dream-life number.
  • Put your quit number somewhere you see it every day.
  • Check your monthly revenue against it, every month.
