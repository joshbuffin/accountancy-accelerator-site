My story.

"In December 2022 I was exactly where you are now. A full-time job I didn't want, and zero clients."

Before the practice

Growing up

Always wanted my own business

  • At about 12 I was selling sweets and lemonade around school.
  • Accounting and finance degree. That's where I met Patrick.

The safe career

PwC, then a multi-billion-pound group

  • 4 years in audit at PwC, where I trained and qualified.
  • 2 years as Group Financial Accountant at a multi-billion-pound business.
  • A good job and a good salary. It just wasn't my life.

I asked for 3 days of annual leave, months in advance. Turned down. That was the catalyst.

January - November 2022

Three businesses that didn't work

  • Built side businesses on social media: training apps for fitness influencers, then brand deals.
  • Very little success. But I learned websites, landing pages and how to find leads.
  • November 2022: the worst period of my life. Home from a job I didn't want, onto a business with no direction. Lonely.

My dad: "Why don't you start an accounting practice?" Me: "But I absolutely hate accounts."

The sprint: £0 to £5k

Monday 19 December 2022

Day one on the high street

  • Suit and tie, a stack of flyers, a list of places. No clients, no one to show me how.
  • The hardest part was getting out of the car. I'd sit there for 20 minutes. That never went away.
  • "Hi, sorry to bother you. Are you the owner? I've finally made the jump and started my own accounting firm..."
  • Most said no: "I've had the same accountant for 15 years."

The routine

Built around the job

  • Couldn't tell work. Couldn't post on social media.
  • Mon - Wed: 5am to 8am on the business, then work.
  • Fridays: a networking event before work.
  • Saturdays: the high street, my big lead generation day.
  • Lunch breaks: follow-ups from my car. Sometimes a "virtual doctor's appointment" to take a call.
  • Gave up sleep, the gym and my mates. Got in trouble at work for being too tired.

Learning the high street

Turning it into a system

  • Googled "posh towns near me", then cafes on Google Maps to find the independents. Lots of Range Rovers was a good sign.
  • Wilmslow, Alderley Edge, Clitheroe, Harrogate, eventually Nantwich.
  • Arrive 10am, finish 2pm, 3 high streets, about 100 business cards.
  • First clients were cafes and bars, because they were the only ones open at weekends.
  • Same complaint everywhere: "My accountant takes a week to reply."

50 chats= 45 nos, 3 maybes, 2 yeses

February / March 2023

The best moment of my life

  • 3 or 4 weekends in a row without a single yes. Rain, dark mornings, shattered.
  • Driving to work on the M65, asking: is this worth it?
  • A call from George. A minute later a WhatsApp from a high-street contact from 6 weeks earlier. Then an email. Three yeses in about 90 seconds.
  • "Paradise" by Coldplay on. Drove past my junction and up and down the M65 for half an hour.

A Wednesday night

The free tax return at Tiffany's

  • A stressed cocktail bar owner with his tax return hanging over him.
  • I sat in his bar for a few hours and did it for free.
  • Told him the monthly price. "A bit more than I expected... but you've just done all this for free." Signed there and then.
  • Help first, then ask.

Easter Sunday, April 2023

The worst day, and the luckiest

  • A full Saturday in Harrogate: 100 cards, one warm lead. "Come back tomorrow."
  • Drove back at 8am. The cafe was shut. It was Easter Sunday.
  • Stood under a canopy in the rain in my suit for an hour. He'd forgotten.
  • Went back the next Saturday. Waited an hour. He said no in 10 minutes.
  • Walking out, a man grabbed my arm: "Are you an accountant? We're looking for one."

June 2023

Earning more than my job

  • Six months after that first Monday.
  • About £5k a month, from weekends on the high street alone.
  • That should have been the day I quit. I'd just applied for a mortgage, so I stayed a bit longer.

£5ka month in 6 months

Summer 2023

The client that changed everything

  • The man from the Harrogate cafe signed at £1,450 a month.
  • "Earning enough not to care if I lost my job."
  • Still a client today, now at £2,250 a month.
  • Not luck. Three trips, an hour in the rain and an hour waiting. I put myself there enough times.

After the sprint

Later in 2023

Handed in my notice. Patrick joins.

  • Told Patrick in Bali: "I'm on to something pretty good here."
  • He tried the high street in Newcastle and sat in his car for half an hour.
  • Me: "The hard bit is the accounts prep." Him: "No, that's the easy bit."
  • I did sales full time. He did client management full time.

Months 9 - 18

Freedom

  • Ran the firm from Morocco, Dubai and Bali. Clients didn't mind.
  • Passed six figures.
  • Finally allowed to post: first TikTok got 63 views. About 500 videos since.

Today

Clearcrest

  • A fully remote team. Our first proper hire was one of my best mates.
  • Accountant of the Year, Manchester & North West England, Prestige Awards 2025/26.
  • September 2026: a £55k month, every pound reinvested in growth.

None of that happens without the first £5k.

Why I'm telling you this

You have no disadvantage.

  • I had nothing you don't have. I just kept getting out of the car.
  • My mum said the work comes back round in 6, 9, 12 months. She was right.
  • What I'd do differently: tell my mates sooner, chase less (twice, then stop), and write a note after every conversation.
  • Your only job for the next 3 to 6 months: reach your quit number. The next video shows you how.
