01

You are in control

  • I give you the frameworks and the playbooks. You do the work.
  • I will never let you stay stuck. But I won't build it for you.
  • Your result comes down to what you do with your hours.

02

About 10 hours a week

  • That's what most people have alongside a full-time job.
  • 10 hours a week is enough to change your life, if you spend it right.

03

A 3 to 6 month sprint

  • This is the hardest part of the whole journey.
  • It has a start date and an end date. Once it's done, you're free.
  • Pick both dates now.

04

What you'll give up

  • For a while: fewer gym sessions, less cooking, saying no to nights out.
  • I gave up sleep, the gym and my mates. I pushed it too far and got in trouble at work for being tired. Don't go that far.
  • Respect your family. Agree the non-negotiables with them before you start.

05

It won't feel like it's working at first

  • I had runs of 3 or 4 weekends without a single yes.
  • My mum told me the work comes back round in 6, 9, 12 months. She was right.
  • Good enough beats perfect. Most of your attention goes on finding clients.

Before the next video

Decide what you'll give up.

  • Write down your sprint start date and end date.
  • Write down 3 things you'll give up until you hit your quit number.
  • Tell the people closest to you what you're doing and why.
