Level 6 - Pricing & Proposals · Video 8

Keep it simple.

Put yourself in the founder's seat: successful, growth-focused and busy. They do not want to read through a long PDF.

A long PDF is too busy, and it adds sales friction. The whole point of the sales process is to reduce that friction. Proposals get lost in a long PDF.

Long PDFOne plain email

Hi,

Thanks again for your time. As discussed, please find below a breakdown of your proposal:

The will be £XXX + VAT from . This will include:

  • Monthly bookkeeping
  • VAT returns
  • Directors Payroll
  • Year end accounts
  • Corporation tax return
  • Confirmation statement
  • Ongoing tax planning advice
  • Direct access to Patrick and I via whatsapp, email and mobile.

for £XXX + VAT, per filing.

The cost to file your current accounts and tax return will be £XXX + VAT.

but I always appreciate it if you can let me know your thoughts either way.

Best,
Josh
