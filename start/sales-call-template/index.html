Level 7 - Sales · Video 4

Your sales call template.

  • Keep it to 30 minutes.
  • Open with: "Tell me about your business and why you reached out today." Note the pain point.
  • Ask your connectors and scope the work. Let them talk.
  • Link their pain points to how you work.
  • "Do you have any questions for me?"
  • Price last, then agree a start date.

Josh's tip: write the pain point down the moment they say it. You'll link back to it later.
