Fix the bottleneck.

Wear one hat at a time.

01

There's always a bottleneck

  • Your business is a hose pipe. Growth stalls wherever there's a kink.
  • Fix one and the next appears. That's progress, not failure.

02

How to find yours

  • Whatever is stressing you out is your bottleneck.
  • At Start it's nearly always the same one: not enough leads.

03

One hat at a time

  • Sales hat, accountant hat, admin hat. Don't wear them all at once.
  • Give each its own time slot. Saturday was my sales day. The accounts fitted round it.

04

Don't fix everything

  • Overwhelm is the biggest reason people stall.
  • Pick one thing. Fix it. Then move on.

Your task

Name your bottleneck.

  • Write down the one thing holding you back right now.
  • Decide which hat you're wearing in each of your 10 hours.
