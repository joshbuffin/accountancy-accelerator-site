Hire your first outsourced worker when your bookkeeping is more than 2 hours a week.

The rule

Hire your first outsourced worker when your bookkeeping passes 2 hours a week.

Even if that means paying for a bit more time than you need at first, and they are a little underutilised. Start early anyway.

Why early

Training is easiest when you both have capacity.

  • It is much easier to train someone when you have the space and they have the space.
  • Get in as early as possible to make the whole training process easier.

They can do admin too

Give them some admin as well.

An outsourced worker can also do admin for you. If they've got two hours of bookkeeping a week, give them some admin too: saving things down, organising something for you, whatever you need.

For example:

BookkeepingEarly accounts prepClient onboardingInvoice chasingAuthorisation codes

Working with people

Get used to working with people.

  • Getting used to using Slack.
  • Getting used to building out a way of working with people.
