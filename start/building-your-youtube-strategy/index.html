Level 5 - Lead generation

Find what works, then film your version.

On YouTube people click, then watch. So the title and thumbnail do the heavy lifting, and that is where your research starts. Don't guess what might work. Look at what already has.

The method

Five steps, in this order.

  1. Install the two toolsvidIQ and 1of10, as Chrome extensions. Chrome works best. Links at the bottom.
  2. Find the creatorsSearch the questions your clients would ask. Mine are "save tax", "UK tax", "UK accountant", "tax for small businesses". Note who keeps showing up.
  3. Open their channel and load a yearGo to the creator's own page, not the search results, and scroll to load the last year of videos. Then sort by best performing.
  4. Spot the outliersThe scores show how each video did against the others on the page. 1of10 is about videos doing far better than that channel's size would suggest.
  5. Log it and make it yoursIf you like the title and thumbnail and could make that video for your clients, add it to your sheet.

Making it yours

Copy the idea, not the video.

Titles and thumbnails

  • Find ones that work for other people, put your face on it, then change it a little bit
  • Don't underestimate how important a good title and thumbnail are
  • Decide the title and thumbnail text before you write a word of the script

Your niche, your angle

  • Take the general idea and make it for your clients: if a video lists ways to take money out of a company, make your own version for your niche
  • Research what business owners click on, because they are your clients
  • Build a bank of 20 to 30 before you pick, so you can see what really does better
