The profitable proposal.

  New email

Hi,

Thanks again for your time. As discussed, please find below a breakdown of your proposal:

1The monthly fee will be £XXX + VAT from [start date]2. This will include:
  • Monthly bookkeeping
  • VAT returns
  • Directors Payroll
  • Year end accounts
  • Corporation tax return
  • Confirmation statement4
  • Ongoing tax planning advice
  • Direct access to Patrick and I via whatsapp, email and mobile.
5Self assessments can be purchased separately for £XXX + VAT, per filing.
6The cost to file your current accounts and tax return to bring everything up to date will be £XXX + VAT.

If I don't hear back I won't chase you but I always appreciate it if you can let me know your thoughts either way.

Best,
Josh

  1. 1The main focus is the recurring monthly cost.
  2. 2The start date anchors onboarding to a set date. It also gives you scope to change their monthly fee, or their annual fees, if they join later.
  3. 3Services are grouped, not priced separately, so they can't be picked apart.
  4. 4We don't pay for Xero fees and confirmation statement fees.
  5. 5Self assessment is billed as a separate line.
  6. 6We split out catch-up fees separately. We want the main focus to be on the recurring cost to them.

Simple wins. Complicated kills.
