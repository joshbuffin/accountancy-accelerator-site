What’s in. What’s out.

Every extra you pull out of the monthly fee goes straight to your margin. Pulling it out doesn’t hurt conversion: we’ve tested it.

Included

In the monthly fee

  • Monthly bookkeeping
  • VAT returns
  • Directors’ payroll
  • Year end accounts
  • Corporation tax return
  • Filing the confirmation statement
  • Ongoing tax planning advice
  • Direct access to us (WhatsApp, email, mobile)

Pulled out

Out: charged separately

  • Self assessmentsBilled separately, per filing. It’s an extra £300 a year per client, and a spike of cash in April and May.
  • Xero subscriptionsThe client pays. About £30 a month is £360 a year per client, so £3,600 a year across 10 clients. (The one exception: Brightpay payroll, about £3 per company, which we absorb.)
  • Virtual officeAn add-on at £10 a month.
  • Companies House feesConfirmation statement fee (£50 online), incorporations and strike-offs. The client covers their own filing fees.
  • Catch-up workPriced separately. It solved our cash flow and funded our ads.
  • Employee payroll (non-directors)Priced separately as a bolt-on.
  • Ad hoc workLetters, forecasting and so on are quoted separately.

What it does to your bottom line

Take a £240 a month proposal. Fold in Xero, the virtual office and the confirmation statement fee, and you give away £530 a year per client: 18% of the fee.
