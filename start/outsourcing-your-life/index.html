Outsourcing your life.

You have so little time that you need to buy more of it.

01

Outsource your life first

  • Before your business, outsource your life.
  • Example: a meal prep company instead of cooking every night.

02

Know what your time is worth

  • Work out your hourly value: what is an hour of your time worth?
  • Anything that costs less than that, and isn't lead generation, is a candidate to outsource.

03

"I'm too small to outsource"

  • The most common mistake.
  • Start small and part-time. Work up.

04

What I outsourced

  • Add yours: what you outsourced in your own sprint.
