Level 1 - Sprint · Video 5

Creating > chasing.

Don't spend your time chasing leads. Spend it creating new ones.

01

Why chasing doesn't work

  • When you're desperate for a client, you chase and chase.
  • It pushes people away. I did exactly this at the start.
  • You can't force someone to buy.

02

My rule: two chases, then stop

  • Chase 1: the next day or the day after.
  • Chase 2: once more, two weeks later.
  • Then stop, and put that energy into new conversations.

2chases, then stop

03

They come back when they're ready

  • Shake 50 hands and maybe 1 is interested on the day.
  • Another 2 might say yes within 12 months.
  • Some called me 9 months later, the day their accountant let them down again.

04

My best morning

  • 3 yeses in about 90 seconds on the M65.
  • One of them was someone I'd met on the high street 6 weeks earlier. I hadn't chased them for weeks.

05

Make it easy to come back

  • Write a note after every conversation: name, business, did you speak to the owner, follow up or not.
  • It takes 6 seconds. I learned the hard way, walking high streets on Google Maps at night to find a shop I'd forgotten.

Your task

Clear your chase list.

  • List everyone you're currently chasing.
  • Anyone chased twice already: stop.
  • Book that time into new lead generation instead.
