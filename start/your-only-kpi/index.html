Our only KPI is MRR.

Monthly recurring revenue.

  • In Accountancy Accelerator we like to keep things simple and we like to optimise for the long term.
  • MRR is the real figure that gives us freedom: it is predictable and gives us an element of certainty.
  • We use it to make key decisions, like quitting your job, investing in marketing and making your first hire.
  • Everything above MRR is a bonus.
  • Thinking longer term, MRR is also the figure that drives the value of your practice, should you ever wish to sell.
  • On sales calls, we always push the prospect to a monthly recurring package, regardless of the size of the company.
  • Everything else (deposits, catch-up fees, pieces of advice) is a tool we use to drive MRR.
