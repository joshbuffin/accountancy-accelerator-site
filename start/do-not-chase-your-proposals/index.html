Don't chase.

  1. 1

    Chasing kills deals.

    Chasing kills more clients and future deals. Not chasing actually increases your close rate. If someone wants to buy from you, they'll buy from you anyway. Tell them you won't keep chasing: it puts you in a stronger position because they think "this person isn't desperate". You also recognise your own value, not someone needy who chases people.

  2. 2

    It protects your time.

    Your crucial input for winning clients is lead gen: how much marketing you do, how many networking events you go to, how many social posts you do, how much you spend on ads and how good they are. Focus your attention there, not on chasing people.

  3. 3

    It protects your head.

    Stop tying your emotions to the yeses. Tie them to your inputs (lead generation), not to things you can't control.

  4. 4

    They will come back.

    Every client has deadlines. If they need your services they'll come back, whether that's their year end, a return, a self assessment, or the accountant they don't like messing something up.

"If I don't hear back I won't chase you but I always appreciate it if you can let me know your thoughts either way."
