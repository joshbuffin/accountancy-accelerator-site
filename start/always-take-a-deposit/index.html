The deposit locks them in.

What counts as a client

Saying yes or signing an engagement letter doesn't really hold anyone to anything. The deposit does.

  • Deposit paid = officially your client
  • From then on, you're in control of that client

You set the deposit

Use it as loosely as your position needs.

  • Tight on cash? Take a bigger deposit, such as 50%, to get cash in.
  • Prioritising MRR and cash in the bank? A 10% or 25% deposit may do.
  • It's unique to you and your business.
