Level 9 - Staffing · Video 8

Track their time from day one.

At first

We didn't want to track.

  • We hated being watched in our old jobs.
  • We didn't want that culture in our firm.
  • Tracking felt like a bad place to work.

What went wrong

The horror stories.

  • Staff went on holiday without telling us.
  • We didn't know who was even doing the work.
  • Invoices came in with hours we couldn't check.

Now

It's the right thing to do.

  • Overseas staff expect it. No pushback.
  • We rarely open it. It's just always there.
  • Export time per client and see who you're not making money on.
The ruleEvery outsourced worker is on Monitask from their first day
