Level 9 - Staffing · Video 1

Good people are rare. Look after them.

Good people are really hard to find. When you find them, look after them and protect them.

Make them feel

Valued

Make them feel

Good at what they do

Make them feel

Supported

Make them feel

Like they can progress

Same for UK or overseas, 60k or 20k.

Mindset

Remember what you hate

Think about what you hate (or hated) about where you work or have worked. Keep it at the front of your mind.

The selfish view

Freedom

The right team is what eventually gives you freedom. You can't have it if your staff aren't self-motivated, so look after them to grow.

Mindset

Abundance, not scarcity

Don't hold people back. Upskill them and help them progress. The moment you get protective, you build a toxic work environment.

Real examples

How we do it

  • $10 an hour for our team in the Philippines, because we want them really well paid.
  • Equipment bought when they started, including second screens.
  • AAT training for team members who wanted more.
  • Remote working for client managers. One went to work from Australia. If people want to travel or live elsewhere, we support it, pretty much no matter what.

The ripple

Great people attract great people

Amazing people bring in more through their network, or just by how they talk about their work. You want a team that loves your business enough to bring others in.
