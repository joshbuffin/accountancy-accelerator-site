Concept 2

Cost per lead is a vanity metric.

Everyone starts out chasing the cheapest lead. The further you get into ads, the more you realise it's the wrong number to track.

Cheap lead

£5

Never turns up to the call. You paid £5 for nothing.

Expensive lead

£25

Shows up, signs, pays you every month.

Track this instead

Cost per qualified call that showed up. Somebody who meets your criteria, and actually turned up.

And ultimately this

Cost per signed client. The only number that tells you whether ads are paying for themselves.

Concept 3

A/B testing: change one thing at a time.

Run two versions that are identical except for one thing. If you change three things at once, you'll never know which one made the difference.

TEST

Topic A vs topic B

Same style, same length, different subject.

TEST

Raw vs polished

Same script, filmed on your phone vs properly edited.

TEST

Text vs no text

Same video, with and without captions on screen.

Concept 4

Don't decide too early.

Spending £30 and calling a winner is deciding on noise. Two leads vs zero leads isn't a result, it's a coin toss.

£100per variation, before you look at the data

Concept 5

Go into every round of ads with a hypothesis.

Know what you want to learn before you launch. Then book the time to check whether you were right.

"I think topic A will beat topic B, because my clients ask me about it every week."

BEFORE

Write it down

One sentence. What you expect, based on what you've learned so far.

DURING

Leave it alone

Let it spend. Don't tinker halfway through.

AFTER

Book the review

Put time in your diary for when the ads finish. Were you right? Write down what you learned.

Every round makes the next one smarter. That's the whole point.

Part three

What we've done to optimise.

Optimising for show rate

A lead who doesn't turn up costs you twice.

Once in ad spend, and again in the diary slot you kept free for them.

No more auto-filled emailsLetting the form fill in emails automatically gave us dodgy addresses. Now people type their email in themselves.
WhatsApp remindersSent automatically through GoHighLevel before every call. People read WhatsApp. They don't always read email.

Testing next

Book within 3-4 days onlyWe think calls booked further out were more likely to be no-shows. Keep the gap short while they're still keen.
Email reminders as wellOf 13 recent no-shows, 4 never got the reminder: their number wasn't on WhatsApp.
Confirm, or lose the slotUnconfirmed calls get cancelled by a set deadline, with an easy way to rebook.
Start the conversation straight awayRight after booking: "Can you tell me more about your business and your accounting experience so far?"

Optimising for quality

The topic decides who stops scrolling.

Lead quality is driven by what your video is about. Talk about a problem your ideal client has, and they're the ones who'll stop.

What we've seen

Pain point topicsLower quality leads.
Holding companies, or 7-figure businessesHigher quality leads.

Side note

Sell what they aspire to beSomeone doing £500k a year might not be 7 figures yet. But they want the accountant who works with 7-figure business owners.

Optimising for intent

Ask what they want from the call.

One question on our form tells us how serious someone is before we ever speak to them.

"What are you looking for from this call?"

OPTION 1

Just looking for advice

Lowest intent. Often wants free answers, not a paid service.

OPTION 2

One-off project

Some intent. Worth a conversation, but not recurring revenue.

OPTION 3

Ongoing monthly support

Highest intent. This is who we want more of, and what we'll ask Meta to find next.

Fewer leads. Far less wasted time.

Optimising for cold vs warm

Warm leads are cheaper. Can you grow on cold?

Retargeting people who already know you is cheaper. The real question is whether your ads keep working when you push to people who've never heard of you.

"Have you heard of the business before?"We ask this on our form, and it decides whose calendar they book into.

Yes, they've heard of us

Booked with me

The face of the brand. They already know who I am.

No, they're new

Booked with a team member

So we can compare the numbers for cold and warm side by side.

More ads isn't better.
Different ads are.

Recent guidance from Meta: it wants to see a real variety of styles from you. Five ads that look different will beat 20 versions of the same video.
