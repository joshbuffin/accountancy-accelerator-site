Stage 1

Before they join

A few weeks out.

1

Send the offer letter for signing.

This is signed weeks before the start date, well ahead of everything else below. Send it via a "Signing request" workflow in Legl, using your offer letter template.

2

Request their pre-start details.

Photo ID, proof of address, P45 and National Insurance number (NINO). Request these in the couple of weeks before they start, using the template email.

📧 Template: pre-start details request email

Subject: Before you start - a few things we need

Hi [First name],

We're really looking forward to having you join us on [start date]. Before your first day, please could you send over the following so we can get everything set up:

  • Photo ID (passport or driving licence)
  • Proof of address (dated within the last 3 months)
  • Your P45 from your previous employer
  • Your National Insurance number (NINO)

We'll also be posting out your equipment (laptop, phone, phone case and mouse) so it arrives before your start date - please reply to confirm the best delivery address.

Any questions, just let me know.

Thanks,
[Your name]

3

Post out their equipment.

So it arrives before day one: laptop, phone, phone case and mouse (plus any other kit they need to work). Confirm the delivery address with them first.

Stage 2

The week before

Email and software access. Set up their company email first, because every software invite goes to it, then work down the list.

1

Set up their Google Workspace email.

All software invites are sent here.

2

Invite them to all our software. Send the access email below so they know what's coming and what each tool is for.

Xero: send invite. Once accepted, grant access to clients.

Client Engager: set them up; share the login with them directly.

Slack: send invite; add them to the relevant channels once they've logged in.

Notion: send invite (SOPs, training videos, templates hub).

Loom: send invite (training videos and screen recordings).

Google Drive: send an invite to your central shared folder.

BrightPay: send invite (payroll).

Nest: send invite (pensions).

Legl: a director sends the invite (signatures and payments for one-off jobs).

Hubdoc: access usually comes via Xero, or a separate email invite (receipt processing).

Online Agent Services Account (Government Gateway for CT, PAYE, SA and CIS): sorted live with a director on the day-one call (the password expires after 24 hours).

Agent Services Account (ASA) (VAT and MTD IT): sorted live with a director on the day-one call (24-hour password expiry).

3

Set them up in BrightHR and check their holiday allowance is correct.

Send the BrightHR invite and assign the relevant HR documents once accepted. Make sure their holiday entitlement is set correctly for the year.

Stage 3

Day one

Onboarding call (morning).

Keep the first day focused. We deliberately do not train on individual software today, because showing everything at once is overwhelming. Today is about access, people and expectations; software is introduced gradually from here. A welcome email can be sent ahead of the call.

📧 Template: first-day welcome email

Subject: Welcome - your first day

Hi [First name],

Welcome to the team - we're really pleased to have you on board. Here's what your first morning will look like. We'll jump on a call at [time] (link to follow) and cover:

  • Signing your contract
  • Your BrightHR documents and holiday allowance
  • Confirming access to all your software
  • An intro to the team
  • Goal setting and an intro to our PDP (Personal Development Plan) process
  • A quick tour of our Notion SOPs so you know where everything lives

Don't worry about learning the software yet - we'll introduce that gradually over your first few weeks. Just make sure you can log in to your new email before we speak.

See you [day],
[Your name]

1

Sign the contract.

The employment contract is sent and signed on day one, using your employment contract template.

2

BrightHR documents and holiday allowance.

Walk through and assign their HR documents. Show them their holiday allowance and explain how to request leave in BrightHR.

3

Confirm software access.

Check they can log in to each system. Sort the two Gateway / ASA accounts live (24-hour password expiry).

4

Intro to the team.

5

Run through the key Slack channels.

Show them where team conversations happen and which channels to follow. The accountants' team channel is the best place for any questions they have.

6

Goal setting and the Personal Development Plan (PDP).

Introduce how we work on their development together. The PDP is how we set goals with every employee, review them, give feedback, and give them the chance to give us feedback too. We catch up monthly to begin with, then move to quarterly once they're settled. For their first month, their only goal is to build relationships with the team and get used to our software and processes. Use the PDP template to track goals and feedback.

Stage 4

First month

Guided projects.

1

Their work lives in a shared Google Sheet task list.

Copy your first-month task list template into a new sheet for the new starter.

2

Each task has an assigned manager.

They walk the new starter through the task and then answer any queries.

3

Everyone has oversight of the shared list.

The whole team can see what the new starter has on their plate and where they're up to.

4

Software and skills are introduced gradually.

They are coached on everything first. The Notion SOPs and Loom videos are supplementary back-up, not the primary training.

5

Admin and bookkeeper support.

Where possible, avoid the new starter using admin and bookkeeper support (except for bookkeeping work) in the first few months, so they can develop a thorough understanding of each task and process. For example: payroll processing and set up, self assessment working papers, accounts working papers and catch-up work.

Stage 5

First three months

Building a portfolio.

1

A new Client Manager won't have a portfolio to start with.

2

For the first three months they assist other Client Managers.

They support other portfolios in the background, gaining valuable training and experience.

3

They build their own portfolio over time.

Any clients we onboard during those three months go into the new Client Manager's portfolio, so they gradually build their own book.

Stage 6

Probation review

3 months.

1

The probation period is 3 months.

2

Hold a review meeting at the end of the 3 months to confirm pass or fail.
