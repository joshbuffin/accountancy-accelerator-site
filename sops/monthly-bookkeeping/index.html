Monthly bookkeeping.

How monthly bookkeeping is planned, prepared, reviewed and followed up, plus how to hand a new bookkeeping client over to the bookkeeper.

Stage 1

Planning

1

The bookkeeping team drafts the schedule for the upcoming month, one week before the start of the month.

The schedule should prioritise clients with VAT deadlines, weekly reports and specific commitments.

SHEETMonthly bookkeeping schedule: exampleGoogle Sheet · example dataOpen ↗
2

The accountant reviews the schedule and checks for any clients that are in Client Engager but not on the schedule (as shown in the Loom video above).

3

The preparer sends the schedule to the reviewer for approval via your team chat.

4

Once it's finalised, confirm to the rest of the team that it's prepared via your team chat.

Stage 2

Preparation

1

Process the items in Hubdoc.

2

Reconcile the bank account in Xero.

3

Raise any questions with the reviewer in the client-specific channel (if applicable to your firm).

4

Prepare the bookkeeping report and send it to the reviewer.

5

For any clients finishing their VAT quarter, review the bills awaiting payment in Xero and send these to the reviewer as a separate document.

6

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.

Stage 3

Review the bookkeeping report

1

Once reviewed, send the queries and the report to the client. Before you do, check:

Everything has been actioned in Hubdoc.

Nothing on the report can be reconciled without an invoice.

Bills awaiting payment, in case there are open bills relating to the transactions you're querying.

The comments on the report make sense. (If you don't understand them, the client probably won't either.)

2

Feed back any review comments to the bookkeeper via the relevant team chat channel.

3

Send the report to the client.

If it's only a couple of queries, you can send them in the body of an email. If it's more than a few, send them as an Excel attachment.

4

Mark the phase on the job card as completed and roll forward the job.

Stage 4

Follow up

1

Once a response has been received from the client, the accountant messages the bookkeeper in the client-specific chat and forwards on the relevant emails.

2

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.

Stage 5

Onboarding a new bookkeeping client

1

The accountant schedules a call with the bookkeeper to run them through the client, go through some transactions on screen and answer any questions the bookkeeper may have.

2

On the call, the bookkeeper shares their screen and completes the bookkeeping notes for that client. Save these in your bookkeeping notes folder on your shared drive.
