Self assessments.

Stage 1

Planning call

1

Before the end of the personal tax year, email all clients inviting them to schedule a personal tax planning call.

2

Before the call, complete the template working file for the client. This includes:

Copying in their notes page from the prior year and updating it for the current year.

Making notes of their current year income and dividends (if you have access to this in Xero, or their pay information).

3

During the call, add any notes to the tab in the template working papers.

4

If there are any action points after the call, send the client an email summary.

5

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.

Stage 2

Information request

1

After the end of the personal tax year, email all clients requesting the relevant information so you can begin preparing their self assessment.

Use your knowledge of the client and the self assessment notes page to amend the template as relevant.

2

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.

Stage 3

Preparation

1

Complete the workings file.

↑ The workings template is in Stage 1, step 2.
2

Prepare the self assessment in Xero Tax.

3

Send it to the reviewer using the template email. Add in the payment details so the reviewer can double-check these as well.

4

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.

Stage 4

Review

1

The reviewer reviews the self assessment and sends back review comments in the working papers.

2

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.

Stage 5

Signing and invoicing

1

Send the self assessment for signing using the template email. The email includes:

A link to schedule a call to discuss the self assessment, should the client wish.

A link to sign the self assessment using Legl.

DOCXSending self assessment for signing: template emailWord document · 13 KBDownload ↓
2

If they need invoicing, let whoever handles invoicing know.

✍️ We send the self assessment through Legl with a payment link included, so the client signs and pays in one step. We only file once the Legl link is fully completed, including both signature and payment.
3

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.

Stage 6

Submission

Once signed off by the client.

1

Save the self assessment and workings in Client Engager (CE).

2

File the self assessment.

3

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.
