Stage 1

Accountant's month end checklist

The checklist is completed by the manager before month end, ready for the month end progress call on the first working day of each month.

1

Check all bookkeeping deadlines in CE have been met for the prior month.

All job cards should be completed and up to date for weekly and monthly bookkeeping clients.

2

Review and send out any confirmation statement emails for the following month plus two weeks.

Check through the confirmation statement job in CE and send emails asking clients to confirm their Companies House details are up to date. Use the template email.

3

Review and send out any tax planning call invites for the month.

Make sure the calendar link is up to date.

4

Review and send out any corporation tax payment reminders for the following month.

Review each client with a corporation tax deadline in the next month. Open their Government Gateway account and check whether the tax is still unpaid. If it's still unpaid, send them a reminder email.

5

Review the bookkeeping schedule.

Check you're happy with the bookkeeping schedule for the following month. Have all your VAT deadlines been prioritised? Do you have any upcoming meetings you'd like bookkeeping prioritised for? Pass any feedback to the bookkeeping team so changes can be made before the start of the following month.

6

Send all chasers for annual accounts and self assessment information requests.

7

Request details from any limited company whose financial year has just ended.

Use the checklist and template email below, and scan the client's Xero account, to request what you'll need to start preparing their year end accounts.

Stage 2

Monthly planning call

Senior management.

1

Onboarding handover process (last week of the month):

The list of clients being onboarded is finalised, and the payroll sheet is updated.

Finalised discovery call notes are uploaded to CE.

Fathom notetaker notes are uploaded to CE.

Dates for all services are reviewed together on a call, in CE.

The handover email is drafted.

The email is reviewed on the call and sent to the client by the Head of Accounts.

2

Review any annual leave in the month to make sure you're correctly resourced for key deadlines.

3

Review actual vs budget.

This should be prepared before the call.

4

Review outstanding invoices.

Set the balance sheet date to the last working day of the month, to allow for the one-day Xero delay.

5

Approve and send any draft invoices.

6

Finalise the focus areas for the month.
