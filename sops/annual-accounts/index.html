Stage 1

Tax planning call

1

Send an email offering a tax planning call 2 months before the company's year end. Use the template email.

Keep calls to 30 minutes, but allow 15 minutes for overrun time (to protect your diary).

DOCXTax planning call: template emailWord document · 11 KBDownload ↓
2

Plan for and host the call with the client. Use the working papers template.

Before the call: make some client-specific notes in the Tax planning call agenda tab of the working papers. Report key figures such as revenue year to date, profit year to date, cost analysis, comparisons to the prior year and the estimated tax liability.

During the call: take notes in the Tax planning call agenda tab of the working papers.

After the call: save the Fathom notes from the call.

XLSXWorking papers templateExcel workbook · used throughout this SOP · 209 KBDownload ↓
3

On the same day, follow up the call with an email summary of what was discussed. Check your email covers:

Any information you said you would provide during the call.

Any key dates the client should be aware of.

Any points either of you need to action.

Stage 2

Information request

Send this straight after the client's year end.

1

Review the Accounts jobs section in Client Engager (CE) for any clients whose year end has just finished.

This is done as part of the month end procedures.

2

Scan the prior year accounts to see what you might need from the client. Use the checklist as guidance on what to consider for each balance sheet line.

3

Email the client requesting the details for the accounts. Use the template email.

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.

Stage 4

Reviewing the accounts

1

The reviewer completes the review, and completes the review phase and its section of the CE checklist.

2

Review comments are documented in the Review tab of the working papers and sent back to the preparer.

3

If applicable, the preparer actions the review comments and re-submits the accounts to the reviewer until the accounts are final.

Repeat these steps until final sign-off is given.

4

The reviewer confirms to the preparer once the review is complete.

5

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.

Stage 5

Sending for signing

1

Send the "accounts for signing" email. Use the template email.

2

Send the accounts and tax computations to the client for signing through Xero Tax.

3

Check whether you need to raise an invoice for the accounts. If so, let whoever handles invoicing know.

4

Re-lock the dates in Xero (if any adjustments have been made).

Xero Central: Set up and work with lock dates ↗

Stage 6

Client call

1

Have the call with the client (if applicable). Run through the agenda with the client while sharing the accounts on screen. The template agenda is in the working papers.

2

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.

Stage 7

Submission

Once signed off by the client.

1

Submit the accounts and tax return via Xero.

Hold off if they have booked a call
▶ Filing the Accounts and CT600 in Xero Tax: this is covered in the same video as preparing them. Watch it in Stage 3, step 2.
2

Double-check the dates are locked in Xero as at the year end date.

3

Save all the documents to Client Engager. Items to save:

Signed accountsCorporation tax documentsTBGL detail reportWorking papersRelevant docs
4

Make notes in the Corporation Tax job card in Client Engager. Fill out the notes on the corporation tax reminder service. If there is no corporation tax to pay, you can roll forward the corporation tax job too.

5

Mark the phase on the job card as completed.
