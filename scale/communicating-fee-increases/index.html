Level 6 - Pricing & Proposals

Raise your fees. One client at a time.

Our fundamentals

1

Review

  • Download all your clients' fees into Client Engager. If they aren't there, add them as you do this.
  • Compare them to your minimum fee structure and look for key discrepancies.
  • Use Xero to see how revenue or transactions have changed over the last year.
  • Decide what each client's fee should be.
2

Schedule

  • Decide when you'll increase each client's fees.
  • Batches, not all in one go.
  • Anyone whose fees increase gets 3 months' notice.
3

Communicate

EmailPhone callVoice noteWhatsApp
  • Justification? Some need it, some don't. Unique to each client.
