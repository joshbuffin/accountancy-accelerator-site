Level 4 - Niche

Could you hand this client to your team?

One of the biggest things that gives you clarity over whether to work with a client.

  • Cash onlyMessy records
  • Values price over serviceRace to the bottom
  • Wants to hide incomeYour name on it
  • Won't use technologySlow, manual work
  • Doesn't show up to the sales callWon't show up later either
  • Sends blunt messages when enquiringHow they start is how they stay
  • Won't move to XeroXero only
  • Won't meet your minimum feesBe stricter on minimum pricing

Not everyone and anyone.

The handover testSometimes you put up with certain things. But if you think ‘I could not hand this over to my team’, that’s your sign: don’t engage, or start to disengage.
