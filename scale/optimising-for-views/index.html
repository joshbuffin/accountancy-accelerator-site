Targeting and testing

Keep targeting broad

Give Meta as much data as you can. Think entrepreneurship and business owners.

Age and gender

Start with a view, such as 25 to 45. Then let your own data decide.

Retarget warm people

Aim ads at people who liked, shared, followed or commented. Warmer leads are cheaper and easier to close.

Static vs video

Test your best video as a static. Like for like.

Watch out

Good views only

Quality beats vanity. Views from random non-followers can train the algorithm on the wrong people, so be careful with trial reels.

Make every ad as different as you can.
